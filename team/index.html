The MLDE group brings together researchers from various disciplines such as information systems, computer science, mathematics, and geoinformatics, to tackle complex problems in data-driven research. By combining expertise in machine learning, algorithms, data engineering, and application domains, we develop innovative solutions that bridge theoretical foundations with practical applications.

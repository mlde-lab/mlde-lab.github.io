research: |
  <div style="text-align: justify">
      My research interests lie at the intersection of embedded artificial intelligence, IoT, and environmental sensing. I am a research associate in the <a href="https://github.com/TinyAIoT/">TinyAIoT</a> project and a co-founder as well as main contributor of the <a href="https://wiediversistmeingarten.org/">Birdiary</a> project. My work focuses on developing resource-efficient machine learning methods for microcontrollers and edge devices, with particular attention to energy efficiency, compact models, and real-world deployment under strict hardware constraints. Methodologically, I contribute to model and data compression for resource-constrained AI, including compact boosted tree models and trainable quantization for efficient data compression. Central application areas of my research are environmental monitoring and smart city, including bird species and bicycle recognition through smart sensing systems. I am also interested in citizen science and in designing accessible digital tools that connect low-power AI technologies with participatory data collection.
    </div>
